About the Library Management System

Welcome to the Library Management System (LMS), a platform designed to streamline the library experience for students. Our LMS allows you to:

  • Search for books and resources available in the library
  • Borrow, renew, and return books with ease
  • Stay updated with the latest additions to the library
  • Access your borrowing history and manage your account

If you have any questions or need assistance, please feel free to contact the library staff directly.
